# ACH Debit Authorization (blank)

A blank debit authorization. The company name is a field, not letterhead. Work the list below. Progress saves in this browser. [Download the printable blank](/resources/ach-debit-authorization.md).


<div class="ix" data-ix="checklist" data-id="res-ach"></div>

Cypress Command worksheet. Copy it per property. The company name is a blank you fill with the legal entity that will originate the debit. Do not leave a prior owner's name, a bank's sample, or another center's letterhead on the form you hand out.

This is operator context for collecting an authorization. It is not legal advice, and it is not a completed authorization. Have your bank, your ACH processor, and your counsel review the wording before anyone signs. Amounts and timing belong in the lease or a written schedule, not invented on this page.

## Authorization

I authorize the Company named above to start ACH debit entries to the checking account below, and to debit that account for amounts the Company and I have agreed in the lease or in a written schedule. I agree that ACH entries to my account must comply with U.S. law.

## Before this blank leaves the office

- [ ] Company legal name is the entity that will originate the debit
- [ ] A prior owner's name, logo, or account is not on the page
- [ ] Amounts and dates live in the lease or a schedule, and the form points at that writing
- [ ] The revocation contact is a real address or inbox
- [ ] Counsel and the bank or processor have reviewed this wording for your originator agreement
- [ ] The signed original is filed with the lease, and the copy you keep does not become the next tenant's form

## When a debit comes back

Do not retry a returned debit because the form exists. A return reopens the rent. An authorization dispute is a stop: keep the form, do not re-present, and hand the file to the person who owns disputes. What a later notice must say is the lease plus counsel.

## The practical next step

1. Write the originator's legal name in the company blank. Leave every prior name off the page.
2. Have counsel and the bank or processor read the wording before the first signature.
3. File the signed form with the lease. Do not reuse it as the next tenant's form.
4. When a debit returns, reopen the rent. Do not retry it because the form exists.
