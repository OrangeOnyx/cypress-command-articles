## Day-one questions

If you cannot answer these from the file, you are still unpacking the center. Write the gap. Do not invent the answer.

- [ ] Where does the rent roll disagree with the executed leases?
- [ ] Which dates in the next 24 months change rent, an option, or a termination right?
- [ ] What is vacant, what is dark, and what is only occupied on the roll?
- [ ] Which categories would a shopper expect here, and which are missing?
- [ ] What did the center recover last year, and which leases block a recovery?
- [ ] What is the condition of roof, HVAC, lot, and life safety, and who pays for each?
- [ ] Which vendors are on site, and which certificates lapse inside 90 days?
- [ ] What does ownership receive every month, and who writes it?
- [ ] Where does the lease put a repair, and where has practice drifted?
- [ ] What would strain a loan covenant or an insurance condition this quarter?
- [ ] What is the after-hours path, and when did someone last walk it?

## What does not belong on this shelf

- A filled handover from a named center
- A signed authorization that already has an account number
- A rent roll
- Someone else's course outline, pasted in to save a draft

## The practical next step

1. Answer the day-one questions from the file, and write down the gaps.
2. Open the worksheet that matches the gap. Do not open all of them.
3. Read the series article when you need the reason, not another blank.
4. Keep named center files and signed forms off this shelf.
