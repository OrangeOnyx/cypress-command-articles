# Owner reporting templates

Blanks for telling an owner what changed, what it cost, and what decision is waiting. Use the same sections every month. A new format is a new mystery.

This is operator context. It is not a financial statement, an audit, or legal advice. Numbers should tie to the rent roll and the general ledger. When they do not, say so in the variance line instead of smoothing them.

## How the cadence fits together

| When | Which blank | What the owner should be able to do after reading it |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 of a new assignment | Exception memo | Know what is broken, missing, or unsafe |
| End of the first month | First-30-days note | Know what stabilized and what did not |
| Every month after | Monthly report | Approve, question, or decide the items in the decision box |
| Every quarter | Quarterly review | See mix, capital, and risk on a longer clock |
| Any week | One-page dashboard | See direction without opening the file |

## Quarterly review

Add these to the monthly report. Do not replace it.

- [ ] Tenant mix: categories you have, categories you are missing, and which expirations make a change possible
- [ ] Capital: the next four quarters, in dollars, with the jobs you are willing to defer
- [ ] Recovery: what the leases let you bill, and where the last reconciliation was disputed
- [ ] Hold period: anything in operations that changes a refinance or a sale conversation
- [ ] Three risks that are not on the monthly dashboard, each with an owner

## KPI glossary

Use these words the same way in every report.

| Word | Meaning on this center |
|---|---|
| Leased occupancy | Area with an executed lease divided by leasable area. Say if dark space is still in the numerator. |
| Economic occupancy | Billed rent divided by the rent the roll would show if vacant area were at your stated market. Show the market assumption. |
| Delinquency | Open charges past the grace the lease gives, not "feels late." |
| Recovery ratio | Billed recoveries divided by the expenses the leases allow you to bill. Say which expenses you left out. |
| NOI | Income minus operating expenses, before debt. Say if a one-time item is in the figure. |
| Variance | Actual minus the budget line, with the rule for what you explain. |

## Mistakes that make a true report useless

- A new outline every month
- A variance with no cause
- Occupancy that hides a dark suite
- Capital described as a repair because the invoice used that word
- A decision buried under a compliment
- Numbers that do not tie, presented as if they do
