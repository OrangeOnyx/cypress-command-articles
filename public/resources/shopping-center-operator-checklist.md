# Shopping Center Operator Checklist

This is operator context for a blank you fill in. It is not legal, tax, or investment advice. Where a lease, a lender, or a statute decides the next step, the worksheet records the question and the document. Counsel answers it.

Benchmarks in the last table are ranges to test against the lease, the market, and the lender. They are not a promise that a deal should clear them.

---

## PART 1: ACQUISITION DUE DILIGENCE CHECKLIST

### Phase I: Pre-LOI Screening
- [ ] Confirm property type: open-air, strip, neighborhood, power, lifestyle
- [ ] Verify GLA and anchor/inline split
- [ ] Review asking cap rate vs. market comps
- [ ] Confirm occupancy rate (physical vs. economic)
- [ ] Identify anchor tenants and lease expiration dates
- [ ] Check co-tenancy clause exposure
- [ ] Review trailing 12-month (T12) operating statement
- [ ] Identify deferred maintenance from broker tour
- [ ] Confirm parking ratio (minimum 4.0/1,000 sf)
- [ ] Check zoning and permitted uses

### Phase II: Financial Underwriting
- [ ] Normalize T12: remove non-recurring income
- [ ] Add back owner-managed expenses to market rate
- [ ] Verify CAM recovery ratio (target: 85–95%)
- [ ] Model vacancy at 5–10% stabilized
- [ ] Calculate management fee at 3–5% EGI
- [ ] Confirm CapEx reserve: $0.50–$1.50/sf/yr
- [ ] Stress-test DSCR at 1.25x minimum
- [ ] Model anchor vacancy scenario
- [ ] Confirm debt coverage at market rate financing
- [ ] Build 5-year hold model with exit cap

### Phase III: Lease Review
- [ ] Abstract all leases: base rent, term, options, CAM, exclusives
- [ ] Flag co-tenancy clauses and trigger conditions
- [ ] Identify exclusivity restrictions on future leasing
- [ ] Confirm personal guaranty on all leases
- [ ] Review percentage rent clauses and breakpoints
- [ ] Check ROFO/ROFR provisions
- [ ] Identify lease termination rights
- [ ] Confirm assignment and subletting restrictions
- [ ] Review holdover rent provisions
- [ ] Flag any below-market leases with long remaining terms

### Phase IV: Physical & Environmental
- [ ] Commission Phase I Environmental Site Assessment
- [ ] Review Phase I — flag RECs for Phase II
- [ ] Inspect roof: age, condition, warranty status
- [ ] Inspect HVAC: age, tonnage, maintenance history
- [ ] Review parking lot: condition, ADA compliance, lighting
- [ ] Inspect electrical panels and service capacity
- [ ] Review plumbing and fire suppression systems
- [ ] Confirm ADA compliance: accessible routes, restrooms
- [ ] Review signage: pylon, building, monument
- [ ] Obtain contractor bids for deferred maintenance items

### Phase V: Title & Survey
- [ ] Order ALTA/NSPS survey
- [ ] Review REAs, easements, and CC&Rs
- [ ] Confirm parking easements and shared access
- [ ] Identify encroachments or boundary issues
- [ ] Review title commitment — flag Schedule B exceptions
- [ ] Confirm no mechanics' liens
- [ ] Verify flood zone status (FEMA)
- [ ] Review reciprocal easement agreements with anchors
- [ ] Confirm utility easements and access
- [ ] Order title insurance: owner's and lender's policies

## PART 2: CAM RECONCILIATION CHECKLIST

### Annual Reconciliation Process
- [ ] Pull prior-year CAM budget vs. actual comparison
- [ ] Categorize all expenses: includable vs. excludable
- [ ] Remove capital expenditures from CAM pool
- [ ] Remove management fee if excluded by lease
- [ ] Remove anchor-specific expenses if excluded
- [ ] Apply gross-up calculation for vacant spaces
- [ ] Calculate each tenant's pro-rata share
- [ ] Apply controllable CAM cap (typically 3–5%)
- [ ] Prepare reconciliation statement per tenant
- [ ] Send statements within 90–120 days of year-end

### Common CAM Exclusions to Verify
- [ ] Capital improvements (roof replacement, parking lot reseal)
- [ ] Depreciation on capital items
- [ ] Leasing commissions and tenant improvement costs
- [ ] Marketing fund contributions (if separate)
- [ ] Anchor tenant spaces (if excluded by REA)
- [ ] Costs covered by insurance proceeds
- [ ] Management fee (if lease excludes)
- [ ] Legal fees for lease disputes
- [ ] Income taxes on landlord entity
- [ ] Financing costs and debt service

### Tenant Audit Defense Preparation
- [ ] Maintain all vendor invoices for 3 years
- [ ] Keep CAM ledger reconciled monthly
- [ ] Document gross-up methodology in writing
- [ ] Confirm audit rights window (typically 12–24 months)
- [ ] Prepare summary of includable expense categories
- [ ] Document all exclusions applied with lease citations
- [ ] Maintain allocation methodology for shared costs
- [ ] Keep copies of all reconciliation statements sent

## PART 3: TENANT DEFAULT RESPONSE CHECKLIST

### Monetary Default
- [ ] Confirm default: missed payment, partial payment, or NSF
- [ ] Check lease for notice requirements and cure period
- [ ] Send formal written demand via certified mail
- [ ] Document date of default and amount owed
- [ ] Calculate late fees per lease terms
- [ ] Review acceleration clause — decide whether to invoke
- [ ] Assess tenant's financial condition and history
- [ ] Determine: accept cure, negotiate, or proceed to eviction
- [ ] If the cure period has ended: stop. Hand the notice, the ledger, and the lease to counsel before any possession filing
- [ ] Do not change locks, shut off utilities, or exclude the occupant yourself. Self-help is not an operator step

### Non-Monetary Default
- [ ] Document the specific default (unauthorized use, sublease, etc.)
- [ ] Review lease for cure period (typically 30 days)
- [ ] Send written notice of default with specific cure requirement
- [ ] Photograph or document the default condition
- [ ] Set calendar reminder for cure period expiration
- [ ] Assess whether default is curable or incurable
- [ ] If the default may be incurable: ask counsel whether the lease allows termination, and wait for that answer
- [ ] Document all communications with tenant

### Post-Eviction / Re-Leasing
- [ ] Secure premises immediately after possession
- [ ] Change locks and alarm codes
- [ ] Conduct condition assessment and photograph everything
- [ ] Prepare cost estimate for restoration to leasable condition
- [ ] Pursue guarantor if personal guaranty exists
- [ ] File claim for unpaid rent, damages, and re-leasing costs
- [ ] Engage broker for re-leasing within 30 days
- [ ] Update rent roll and financial model for vacancy

## PART 4: VENDOR MANAGEMENT CHECKLIST

### Vendor Onboarding
- [ ] Obtain W-9 and verify entity type
- [ ] Collect certificate of insurance (COI)
- [ ] Verify minimum coverage: $1M GL, $2M aggregate, $1M auto
- [ ] Confirm landlord named as additional insured
- [ ] Execute written service agreement before work begins
- [ ] Define scope of work, pricing, and payment terms
- [ ] Establish performance standards and KPIs
- [ ] Confirm licensing and bonding requirements
- [ ] Verify workers' compensation coverage
- [ ] Set up vendor in accounting system

### Ongoing Vendor Performance
- [ ] Track COI expiration dates (calendar reminders 60 days out)
- [ ] Conduct quarterly performance reviews for major vendors
- [ ] Document complaints and service failures in writing
- [ ] Compare invoices to contract pricing monthly
- [ ] Conduct annual competitive bid for contracts over $10K/yr
- [ ] Review landscaping: frequency, quality, seasonal schedule
- [ ] Review HVAC: PM schedule, response time, parts markup
- [ ] Review janitorial: frequency, scope, supply costs
- [ ] Review security: coverage hours, incident reporting
- [ ] Review parking lot: sweeping, striping, lighting maintenance

### Vendor Termination
- [ ] Review contract for termination notice requirements
- [ ] Send written termination notice per contract terms
- [ ] Secure all property access (keys, codes, badges)
- [ ] Obtain final invoice and reconcile open balances
- [ ] Document reason for termination in vendor file
- [ ] Transition work to replacement vendor before termination effective date
- [ ] Retain all documentation for 3 years

## KEY BENCHMARKS REFERENCE

| Metric | Target Range |
|--------|-------------|
| Occupancy Cost Ratio (OCR) | ≤12% for inline tenants |
| CAM Recovery Rate | 85–95% |
| Controllable CAM Cap | 3–5% annual increase |
| CapEx Reserve | $0.50–$1.50/sf/yr |
| Parking Ratio | ≥4.0/1,000 sf |
| DSCR Minimum | 1.25x |
| Management Fee | 3–5% of EGI |
| Phase I Cost | $2,500–$4,500 |
| DD Period | 30–60 days |
| CAM Reconciliation Deadline | 90–120 days post year-end |
| Tenant Audit Window | 12–24 months |
| Possession notice | The lease plus local law — confirm the day-count with counsel. Do not reuse a generic number |
