# Shopping Center Handover Checklist

A blank transition file for a shopping center changing owners or managers. Fill every line. Do not mark an item complete without the artifact in hand: a document, a confirmation, an account credential, or a signed acknowledgment.

This is operator context for a blank you fill in. It is not legal, tax, or investment advice. Where a lease, a lender, or a statute decides the next step, the worksheet records the question and the document. Counsel answers it.


Items tagged **[LA]** are a Louisiana example. Items tagged **[LFT]** are a Lafayette Parish / Acadiana example. Replace that layer for another parish or state. No property name, tenant, or account belongs on the master blank — copy it per center.

## How to use this document

This checklist is organized in three time horizons: (1) Pre-Closing Due Diligence — what must be collected, reviewed, and verified before the deal closes; (2) Closing & Day-One Transition — what must be executed at or around the transfer date; (3) First 90 Days — operational handover, integration, and verification.

Each phase is broken into cross-cutting domains: Legal & Corporate, Title & Real Property, Leases & Tenants, Financial / Bookkeeping, Operations, Physical Plant, Insurance & Risk, Regulatory Compliance, Marketing, and Technology. Items marked with [LA] are Louisiana-specific. Items marked with [LFT] are Lafayette / Acadiana specific.

Every checkbox is intended to produce a deliverable: a document, a confirmation email, an account credential, or a signed acknowledgment. Do not mark complete without the artifact in hand.

## Phase 1 — Pre-Closing Due Diligence

Target completion: before closing / contract effective date. Sellers / outgoing operators are typically obligated to deliver these under the PSA's due diligence clause.

### 1.1 Entity & Corporate Documents

- [ ] Articles of Organization / Incorporation for owner entity (certified copy)
- [ ] Operating Agreement / Bylaws (current, fully executed, with all amendments)
- [ ] Certificate of Good Standing from Secretary of State [LA: louisiana.gov/SOS]
- [ ] EIN confirmation letter (IRS CP-575 or equivalent)
- [ ] Federal tax returns — prior 3 years (Form 1065 / 1120 / 1040-Sch E)
- [ ] State tax returns — prior 3 years
- [ ] Authorized signer resolution / consent for sale or transfer
- [ ] List of all members / shareholders with ownership percentages
- [ ] Capital account ledger / member contribution history
- [ ] Prior K-1s for all members (3 years)
- [ ] Any side letters, buy-sell agreements, or transfer restrictions
- [ ] UCC search on entity name (no liens against owner entity)
- [ ] Litigation search — entity as plaintiff or defendant

### 1.2 Title, Survey & Real Property

- [ ] Vesting deed (most recent recorded conveyance to current owner)
- [ ] Chain of title — back 20+ years or to subdivision plat
- [ ] Current title commitment / preliminary title report
- [ ] Owner's title insurance policy (existing)
- [ ] All recorded exceptions to title — copies of each
- [ ] ALTA / NSPS Land Title Survey (recent — within 12 months ideal)
- [ ] Topographic survey if available
- [ ] As-built site plan
- [ ] Boundary survey with bearings / distances
- [ ] Legal description (metes & bounds) — verify matches deed
- [ ] Plat / subdivision map showing parcel(s)
- [ ] Mineral rights status [LA: critical — minerals often severed from surface]
- [ ] Servitudes of passage / utility servitudes [LA civil law term for easements]
- [ ] Air rights / subsurface rights documentation if applicable

### 1.3 Loan, Mortgage & Lender Documents

- [ ] All current notes (promissory notes) on the property
- [ ] Mortgage / Deed of Trust / [LA: Act of Mortgage] for each loan
- [ ] Loan agreement and any modifications / extensions
- [ ] Most recent loan payoff statement (good for 30 days)
- [ ] Current loan amortization schedule
- [ ] Lender escrow analysis (tax, insurance, reserves)
- [ ] Reserve account balances (replacement, TI/LC, debt service)
- [ ] Lender estoppel / consent to transfer (if assumable loan)
- [ ] Defeasance or prepayment penalty quote (if applicable)
- [ ] UCC-1 filings by lender — review and prepare termination if paying off
- [ ] Cross-collateralization documents (if loan covers other properties)
- [ ] Loan covenants compliance certificates (DSCR, occupancy, etc.)
- [ ] Lender-required reports (operating statements, rent rolls submitted)
- [ ] Guaranty agreements and current status of guarantors

### 1.4 Easements, REAs, CC&Rs & Operating Covenants

REAs (Reciprocal Easement Agreements) and OEAs are the single most-overlooked risk area in shopping center transitions. Read every page.

- [ ] Reciprocal Easement Agreement(s) — full executed copy with all amendments
- [ ] Operating Easement Agreement (OEA) if separate from REA
- [ ] Declaration of Covenants, Conditions & Restrictions (CC&Rs)
- [ ] Cross-parking easements
- [ ] Access / ingress-egress easements
- [ ] Utility easements (water, sewer, gas, electric, telecom, drainage)
- [ ] Stormwater / detention easements
- [ ] Sign easements (pylon, monument, off-premise)
- [ ] Anchor tenant operating covenants (continuous operation, hours, use)
- [ ] Co-tenancy and dark-store clauses affecting property obligations
- [ ] Common area maintenance obligations across multiple parcels
- [ ] REA-mandated insurance requirements and current compliance
- [ ] Approval rights — who must consent to leases, alterations, redevelopment
- [ ] Right of first refusal (ROFR) / right of first offer (ROFO) holders
- [ ] Schedule of all parties with REA approval rights

### 1.5 Zoning, Land Use & Entitlements

- [ ] Current zoning classification — written verification
- [ ] Zoning compliance letter / zoning verification letter
- [ ] Permitted uses under current zoning
- [ ] Variances, special exceptions, conditional use permits granted
- [ ] Site plan approvals (original and amendments)
- [ ] Setback, height, parking ratio, FAR requirements vs actual
- [ ] Non-conforming use status (grandfathered uses)
- [ ] Pending zoning changes that could affect property
- [ ] Adjacent zoning and pending changes [LFT: check Lafayette Consolidated Government]
- [ ] Comprehensive plan / future land use designation
- [ ] Historic designation status (none expected for typical retail strip)
- [ ] Tax Increment Financing (TIF) district participation
- [ ] Economic development incentive agreements [LA: ITEP, RTA, Quality Jobs]

### 1.6 Environmental

- [ ] Phase I Environmental Site Assessment (within 12 months for lender)
- [ ] Phase II ESA if Phase I identified recognized environmental conditions
- [ ] Historical use research (gas stations, dry cleaners, auto repair on site or adjacent)
- [ ] Underground storage tank (UST) registration and status
- [ ] Above-ground storage tank documentation
- [ ] Asbestos-containing materials (ACM) survey [pre-1981 buildings]
- [ ] Lead-based paint survey (commercial use exempt but tenants may trigger)
- [ ] Mold remediation history
- [ ] Radon testing results
- [ ] Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) if construction-active
- [ ] Spill Prevention Control and Countermeasures (SPCC) plan if applicable
- [ ] Wetlands delineation [LA: heightened relevance given Acadiana hydrology]
- [ ] FEMA flood zone designation and current Flood Insurance Rate Map (FIRM)
- [ ] Elevation certificate(s) for each building
- [ ] Letter of Map Amendment / Revision (LOMA / LOMR) if applicable
- [ ] Hurricane / windstorm damage history and repair records [LA]

### 1.7 Engineering, Structural & ADA

- [ ] Property Condition Assessment (PCA) / Property Condition Report
- [ ] Structural engineer reports (any in last 5 years)
- [ ] Roof inspection reports and remaining useful life estimates
- [ ] HVAC inventory and condition assessment per unit
- [ ] Parking lot condition assessment (pavement, striping, lighting)
- [ ] Drainage / stormwater system condition
- [ ] ADA Title III accessibility audit
- [ ] ADA transition plan / remediation schedule
- [ ] Fire suppression system inspection (sprinklers, hoods)
- [ ] Backflow preventer test reports
- [ ] Elevator inspection certificates (if applicable)
- [ ] Generator load-bank test reports (if applicable)
- [ ] Geotechnical / soils report (original construction)

- [ ] Original lease (fully executed)
- [ ] All amendments, addenda, side letters in chronological order
- [ ] Renewal / extension exercises (written notices and acknowledgments)
- [ ] Letter of Intent (LOI) and original term sheet
- [ ] Guaranty agreements (corporate and personal)
- [ ] Guarantor financial statements and credit reports
- [ ] Tenant financial statements (most recent available)
- [ ] Tenant sales reports (if percentage rent or required reporting)
- [ ] Estoppel certificate — current within 30 days of closing
- [ ] Subordination, Non-Disturbance and Attornment Agreement (SNDA)
- [ ] Memorandum of Lease (recorded, if any)
- [ ] Tenant Improvement (TI) / Build-Out agreement and completion certificates
- [ ] TI allowance reconciliation — amounts paid and remaining
- [ ] Commencement Date Confirmation / Delivery Memo
- [ ] Punch list and resolution documentation
- [ ] Certificate of Occupancy for tenant space
- [ ] Certificate of Insurance (COI) — current, with landlord as additional insured
- [ ] Tenant W-9 (rare — typically reverse: landlord W-9 to tenant)
- [ ] Security deposit ledger (amount, form, holding location)
- [ ] Letter of Credit (if used as deposit) — original physical document and renewal status
- [ ] Prepaid rent ledger
- [ ] Default notices issued, cure status, and current standing
- [ ] Co-tenancy clause inventory and compliance status
- [ ] Exclusive use clause inventory (use restrictions binding other tenants)
- [ ] Radius restriction inventory (tenant cannot operate within X miles)
- [ ] Option to expand / contract / terminate inventory with trigger dates
- [ ] Right of First Refusal / First Offer on adjacent space
- [ ] Assignment / sublease history and consents
- [ ] Tenant operating hours requirements
- [ ] Signage rights documentation (pylon position, monument, façade)

### 1.9 Rent Roll & Financial History

- [ ] Certified rent roll as of due diligence date

## Phase 2 — Closing & Day-One Transition

These items execute at, on, or within 5 business days of closing. Coordinate among seller, buyer, title company, and incoming property manager.

### 2.1 Prorations & Closing Statement

- [ ] Rent prorations — current month, by tenant
- [ ] Prepaid rent transfer to buyer
- [ ] Real estate tax proration [LA: parish assessor cycle — verify]
- [ ] Insurance proration / cancellation refund
- [ ] CAM, tax, insurance reconciliations — final true-up at closing or 90 days post
- [ ] Utility prorations and final meter readings
- [ ] Vendor accruals — outstanding work / unbilled services
- [ ] Management fee / leasing commissions accrual
- [ ] Outstanding TI / LC obligations assumed by buyer
- [ ] Lender escrow balance transfer or refund
- [ ] Reserve account transfer

### 2.2 Security Deposits & Letters of Credit

- [ ] Security deposit reconciliation per tenant
- [ ] Cash deposit transfer to buyer's account (or credit at closing)
- [ ] LOC originals delivered to buyer
- [ ] LOC amendments / new LOCs in buyer's name (if required)
- [ ] Buyer assumes obligation to account / refund per lease terms
- [ ] Tenant notification of deposit transfer

### 2.3 Tenant Notification & Rent Direction

- [ ] Joint tenant notification letter (seller + buyer) — signed and mailed
- [ ] Rent direction letter — new payee, address, ACH info, payment portal
- [ ] New W-9 for buyer entity delivered to all tenants
- [ ] ACH change instructions for tenants on auto-pay
- [ ] New COI requirements / additional insured update letter
- [ ] Welcome packet to tenants from new owner / PM
- [ ] Property manager introduction (name, phone, email, emergency line)

### 2.4 Estoppels & SNDAs

- [ ] Estoppel certificate from each tenant — dated within 30 days of closing
- [ ] Discrepancies between estoppel and lease — resolved or carved out
- [ ] Anchor tenant estoppels — extra scrutiny on co-tenancy / continuous op
- [ ] REA party estoppels (if required)
- [ ] SNDAs from lender to tenants (if new financing)
- [ ] Subordination requirements re-confirmed

### 2.5 Insurance Transfer

- [ ] New owner insurance bound effective at closing — binder delivered
- [ ] Lender named as mortgagee / loss payee on new policy
- [ ] REA-required certificate holders added
- [ ] Tenant additional-insured status preserved or replaced
- [ ] Existing policy cancellation effective at closing
- [ ] Pending claims — clarified who controls (typically seller)
- [ ] Tail coverage / extended reporting period (if required)

### 2.6 Banking & Cash Management

- [ ] Operating bank account opened in buyer entity name
- [ ] Security deposit trust account opened (separate from operating) [LA: best practice]
- [ ] Reserve account opened (replacement reserves, TI/LC)
- [ ] Bank signatories filed (managing member, PM, controller as applicable)
- [ ] Wire and ACH instructions documented and distributed internally
- [ ] Lockbox / PO box established for rent receipts (if used)
- [ ] Online banking access provisioned to PM, bookkeeper, owner
- [ ] Positive pay / fraud controls enabled
- [ ] Tenant payment portal configured (DoorLoop, AppFolio, or equivalent)
- [ ] Credit card processing / merchant account established

### 2.7 Vendor Assignment & Notification

- [ ] Service contracts assigned (consents obtained where required)
- [ ] Vendors notified of ownership change in writing
- [ ] New billing addresses provided to each vendor
- [ ] New W-9 from buyer to vendors who owe 1099 reporting
- [ ] Replacement vendors selected where seller relationship not transferable
- [ ] Emergency vendor list distributed (24/7 plumbing, electrical, roof, glass, locksmith)

### 2.8 Keys, Access Codes, Locks

- [ ] Master key inventory — physical handover
- [ ] Sub-master / submaster keys per building / area
- [ ] Suite keys (where landlord retains spares per lease)
- [ ] Mechanical / electrical / roof / janitorial closet keys
- [ ] Alarm codes — main and per suite
- [ ] Gate / dumpster enclosure codes
- [ ] Management office locks rekeyed at closing
- [ ] Common area door / restroom keys
- [ ] Roof access keys / hatch codes
- [ ] Sign / monument access keys
- [ ] Key log with chain of custody
- [ ] Decision: rekey master after closing (recommended)

### 2.9 Utility Transfer

- [ ] Electric — common areas, parking lot lighting, signage [LFT: LUS or SLEMCO]
- [ ] Natural gas (if applicable)
- [ ] Water / sewer — landlord meters [LFT: Lafayette Utilities System]
- [ ] Trash service (if landlord-paid)
- [ ] Telecom / internet for management office
- [ ] Irrigation water (if separate meter)
- [ ] Final meter readings recorded
- [ ] Deposit refunds requested where applicable
- [ ] New accounts opened in buyer entity name
- [ ] Autopay configured

### 2.10 Mail, Address & Public-Facing Items

- [ ] USPS mail forwarding from prior owner address (if different)
- [ ] Property mailing address confirmed (suite for management office)
- [ ] Registered agent updated [LA: Sec of State filing]
- [ ] Property website / domain transferred or redirected
- [ ] Google Business Profile claimed / transferred
- [ ] Social media account access transferred or new accounts created
- [ ] On-site signage updated (management contact, leasing inquiries)

## Phase 4 — Bookkeeping & Accounting Handover

### 4.2 Accounts Receivable

- [ ] AR aging at closing — by tenant

- [ ] Write-off policy for uncollectible accounts

### 4.3 Accounts Payable

- [ ] Unbilled work / accrued services identified

### 4.4 CAM Reconciliation Status
